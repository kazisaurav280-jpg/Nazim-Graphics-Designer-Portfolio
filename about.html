Who AM I

Know About me

I'm Nazim, a visual designer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I grew up sketching movie posters in the back of my school notebooks, and that love for bold, story-driven images still shapes everything I make.

For the past six years I've worked with agencies, media brands and small businesses on posters, brand identities, logos, packaging and social media campaigns. My work blends retro print textures, halftones and hand-drawn type with clean, modern layouts.

Every project starts with listening: what you want to say, who you're talking to, and how you want people to feel. When I'm not designing, I'm hunting for old film posters.
