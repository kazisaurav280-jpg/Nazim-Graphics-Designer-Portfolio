About Me

I'm Nazim, a visual designer from Dhaka with over six years of turning ideas into bold, memorable visuals. I design posters, brand identities, logos and social media content for startups, agencies and creators. My style mixes retro print textures with clean, modern layouts, and every project starts with the story you want to tell.
