Summer Premiere Poster

Retro stripes and a bold portrait, made to stop the scroll on launch week.

Overview

A summer premiere needs one image that works everywhere: a phone thumbnail, a billboard and a social post. The idea was to pair a mysterious portrait with loud retro stripes so the poster reads instantly at any size.

The blindfold keeps the story a secret, and the warm, sun-faded palette sets the season before anyone reads a word.

The Brief

Create launch key art that feels nostalgic, hints at the plot and stays readable as a tiny thumbnail.

The Idea

A blindfolded hero framed by 70s stripes. The mystery comes from what she can’t see, the energy from the pattern.

The Outcome

A single master layout that adapts to portrait, square and landscape crops without losing the focal point.
